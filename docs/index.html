Frequently Asked Questions

Is Emergency Dental Service a 24 hour dentist in Chicago?

Yes. We are a 24 hour dentist serving Chicago, IL. Call 773-243-4575 at any time of day or night and our team will help you get urgent dental treatment as quickly as possible.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out, broken or chipped tooth, swelling or a dental abscess, bleeding that won’t stop, and injuries to the mouth or jaw are all dental emergencies. If you’re not sure, call us and we’ll help you decide what to do next.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment?

Yes. We prioritize urgent cases and offer same day appointments for emergency dental visits, including emergency tooth extractions and emergency root canals.

What should I do while I wait to see the dentist?

Rinse your mouth gently with warm water, hold a cold compress against your cheek to reduce swelling, and take an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed on the label. If a tooth was knocked out, hold it by the crown (not the root), keep it moist in milk, and call us right away.

Is emergency dental care affordable?

We aim to keep emergency dental care affordable. We’ll explain your treatment options and costs before we begin, and you can call us to ask about payment options and insurance.
